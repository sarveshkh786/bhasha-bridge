A wrong date on a water-cut notice, a dropped digit in a helpline, a fee that changed in translation: these are the mistakes that hurt. Bhasha Bridge lets an AI model translate, then checks with plain code that every date, time, amount, phone number and link is still exactly right before it shows anything.

  • 12 Indian languages Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Urdu, Kannada, Odia, Malayalam, Punjabi, Assamese. Easy-read or standard.
  • Number lock Code, not the model, compares numbers in every script: ४:३० = 4:30, ₹1,20,000 = ₹120000.
  • Your browser, your key Runs as an open agentic website. No server, no account. Any agent site can call it by URL.

Give your agentic site 12 languages with one line

Bhasha Bridge publishes its agent network under the Open Agentic Web spec. Add its network URL to one of your agent's tools, and allow this origin in your agent-manifest.json:

"tools": ["https://sarveshkh786.github.io/bhasha-bridge/bhasha_bridge"]

"allowed_peers": ["https://sarveshkh786.github.io"]

Your visitor approves the call on a consent card. The network then runs in their browser on their own key and model, and answers with the translation plus a Numbers kept: line, so your page can run the same check. The number lock is a dependency-free module you can copy: js/number-lock.js.

  • agent-manifest.json — the site's manifest
  • bhasha_bridge.network.json — the agent network (public by design)
  • site-actions.js — the four page tools the agent can call

The Shantinagar notice is fictional; its phone number and link are placeholders. Translations are made by the AI model you choose and are not certified for legal or official use.
